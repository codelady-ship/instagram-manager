# Formats (quick reference)

## Reel (15–60 s)

| Part | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 s | Spoken line + big on-screen text + a moving first image. Promise the payoff. |
| Setup | 3–8 s | Why it matters, in one sentence. |
| Body | 8–45 s | 3 steps / 3 points, a new shot every 2–4 s. On-screen text repeats the key words. |
| Payoff | last 5–10 s | The result, the answer, the "after". |
| CTA | last 2–3 s | One ask: save, share, follow for part 2, or comment a keyword. |

Hook patterns (write new words each time):
- Mistake: "Stop doing X if you want Y."
- Result first: show the end, then "here is how".
- Question the viewer has: "Can you study in Hungary for free?"
- Number: "3 free tools I use every day for…"
- Contrast of before and after, shown, not said.

Word budget: about 2–2.5 spoken words per second (30 s ≈ 65–75 words). Captions on screen: max 6–8 words at a time, inside the safe area (not the top 15% or bottom 25%, where buttons cover text).

Shot list format:

```
0-3s  | face to camera, close  | TEXT: "3 free study apps" | "Here are 3 apps..."
3-8s  | screen recording app 1 | TEXT: "1. Anki"           | "First..."
```

## Carousel (6–10 slides, 4:5 portrait 1080 × 1350)

1. Hook slide: big text, max 8–10 words, a clear promise.
2. Problem or context.
3–8. One idea per slide, large font, one visual.
9. Summary or checklist (the most saved slide).
10. CTA: save, share, comment, follow.

Keep the same template on every slide; contrast high enough to read on a phone in sunlight.

## Photo post

One clear subject, natural light, the topic visible in the thumbnail. Text overlay only if it adds meaning.

## Stories (daily contact)

- 3–7 frames a day max.
- One interactive sticker per day: poll, quiz, question box, slider, link.
- Behind the scenes, reposts of the new post with a "new post" note, reviews (with the person's permission).
- Save the good ones into highlights.

## Caption template

```
<Hook line, under 125 characters>

<2-5 short lines: the value, the story or the steps>

<One CTA: save / share / comment WORD / link in bio>

<3-5 hashtags>
```

## Cross-post variants (only when asked)

- **TikTok:** same video, native text and sound, a slightly faster hook, caption shorter.
- **LinkedIn:** the lesson as text: 1-line hook, 3–5 short lines, a question at the end; no hashtag lists.
- **Facebook:** same Reel; caption a bit longer and more personal for local groups.

## Labels

- Paid, gifted or affiliate content: Instagram "Paid partnership" label + a clear word in the caption.
- Realistic AI images or video: "AI info" label.
- Giveaways: follow Instagram's promotion rules; state that Instagram is not involved, give the rules and the end date.
