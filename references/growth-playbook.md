# Growth playbook (organic only)

Facts marked *official* were checked on Instagram/Meta help pages on 2026-11-11. Features roll out unevenly: say "if your app shows it".

## Current limits and specs

| Item | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Hashtags per post or Reel | **max 5**; more than 5 and the post/comment won't go through | official (Help Center "Use hashtags on Instagram") |
| Carousel | up to 20 photos/videos | announced Aug 2024 (press); check in app |
| Reel length | up to 3 minutes | announced Jan 2025 (press); check in app |
| Profile grid preview | 3:4 crop; feed post 4:5 (1080 × 1350) is still safe | announced Jan 2025 (press) |
| Caption preview | about the first 125 characters show before "…more" | observed, not official |
| AI label | toggle **"Add AI label"**, shows as **"AI content"**; needed for photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio made or changed with AI | official |
| Paid partnership label | required when you are paid or gifted by a brand; works in Feed, Stories, Reels, Live, broadcast channels | official |

## Instagram SEO (search inside Instagram)

- **Name field:** name + the keyword people search ("Aysel | Coding Teacher").
- **Caption first line:** say the topic in plain words people type ("free coding course for beginners in Azerbaijani").
- **On-screen text** and spoken words in Reels: say the keyword in the first 3 seconds.
- **Alt text:** one plain sentence with the topic.
- Hashtags: max 5, real topic words; keywords matter more than extra tags.
- Public professional accounts can also show up in Google search; keep the bio clear.

## Trial Reels (official, creators.instagram.com, 2024-12-10)

What it is: a Reel shown **first to non-followers only**. It does not appear in followers' feeds or on the grid unless you share it. Results come after about **24 hours**; you can let Instagram share it to followers automatically if it does well within 72 hours.
Who has it: switch on "Trial" when sharing a Reel, **if your app shows the toggle** (not everyone has it).

Test plan:
1. Same video, 2 different hooks (first 3 s + on-screen text) → 2 trial Reels a few days apart.
2. After 24–48 h compare: views from non-followers, 3-second hold / skip rate if shown, shares, follows.
3. Share the winner to followers; reuse the winning hook pattern.
Never use trials to post the same video again and again (it looks like spam).

## Story sequences (daily contact)

3–5 frames, one goal, one sticker:
1. **Hook frame:** a question or a "behind the scenes" moment.
2. **Value frame(s):** a tip, a result, a short video.
3. **Sticker frame:** poll, quiz, question box, slider or link.
4. **CTA frame:** "DM me 'PLAN'" / "new Reel up" / link.
Ideas: "day in my life", "before → after", "this or that" poll, "ask me anything" box, a client review (with permission), a mini-quiz. Save the best to a highlight.

## Outbound comments (15 minutes a day, by hand)

- Pick 10–15 accounts your audience follows (bigger creators, local pages, peers). No automation.
- Leave 3–5 **real** comments a day: add a tip, share a small experience, or ask a specific question. Never "Nice post 🔥", never "check my page".
- Reply to every comment on your own post in the first hour.

## Collab posts and pitches

- **Collab post:** invite a co-author when sharing (the post appears on both profiles). Best with peers of similar size and the same audience.
- Pitch DM (by hand, to people who share your audience):
  - EN: "Hi <name>! I love your <specific post>. I teach <topic> to <audience>. Idea: a joint Reel on '<topic>' — you do <part>, I do <part>. Want to try?"
  - AZ: "Salam, <ad>! <Konkret post> çox xoşuma gəldi. Mən <auditoriya> üçün <mövzu> öyrədirəm. Təklif: '<mövzu>' haqqında birgə Reel — siz <hissə>, mən <hissə>. Maraqlıdır?"
- Brand deals: ask for the brief, deliverables, dates, usage rights and budget in writing; always the Paid partnership label. A simple rate card = what you offer (1 Reel, 3 Stories), example results from your own Insights, and "price on request".

## Repurpose one idea into a week

One strong idea (a tip, a lesson, a client story) →
- Mon: Reel (the idea in 30–60 s)
- Tue: Stories (poll on the problem + behind the scenes)
- Wed: Carousel (the steps, slide by slide)
- Thu: Stories (answer the questions from the box)
- Fri: Photo or quote post (the key line) or a trial Reel with a new hook
- Weekend: Story recap + link
Cross-post: TikTok (same video, native text), LinkedIn (the lesson as text), Facebook (same Reel, longer caption).

## Competitor / outlier review (from pasted posts)

The user pastes 10–20 posts from 1–3 similar accounts (format, topic, hook line, views or likes). You:
1. Find each account's **median** and mark posts at **2× the median or more** as outliers.
2. For each outlier: hook pattern, format, length, topic angle, CTA.
3. Give 3 ideas for the user in their own words. Never copy captions or videos.
For screenshots, read only what is visible; say what is missing.

## Human-voice check (captions)

Before giving a caption, remove: "In today's fast-paced world", "Unlock", "Elevate", "Dive in", "game-changer", "It's not X, it's Y", triple lists in every line, emoji at the end of every line, empty hype. Prefer: one real detail, short lines, the user's own phrases. Read it aloud; if it sounds like an ad robot, rewrite.

## Key metrics (weekly review)

- **Sends per reach** (shares ÷ reach): the strongest signal that new people will see it.
- Saves per reach, follows per 1000 reach, 3-second hold / average watch time for Reels if shown.
- Rank posts by "× the user's own median"; one week is not a trend.
