## Description
```markdown
Paste your bio, your last posts and (later) your Insights numbers. It runs one honest loop for a small account: audit → pillars → 2-week calendar → drafts → replies → weekly review → next 2 weeks.

You get:
- a profile audit scored /20 with keyword (Instagram search) fixes and 2 new bios
- 3–4 content pillars and a calendar sized to the time you really have
- Reel scripts with scored hooks and shot lists, carousels, Story sequences
- captions with alt text, one CTA and at most 5 hashtags (Instagram's current limit)
- Trial Reels hook tests, a daily comment plan, collab and brand-deal templates
- reply templates in Azerbaijani, Russian and English
- a weekly review led by sends per reach, against your own median
- a competitor review from posts you paste, without copying them

**Where it stops:** it can't see your account and works only from what you paste. It never asks for passwords and never automates likes, follows, comments or DMs. It never suggests bought followers or pods, and it promises no growth numbers. Paid, gifted and realistic AI content gets labelled.
```
